PokéSurvivor moves and their evolutions

There are 93 moves across all 18 types. Your Pokémon attacks on its own with the active one, and when you level up you pick cards to learn new moves or upgrade them up to level 5. 93 of them can evolve: max them out, get the upgrade card they ask for (Power, Vigor, Reflexes…) and a golden evolution card will appear.

Independent passive moves

4 independent slots, 6 levels per passive; the previous total power is preserved. C or R3 chooses a special; V or L3 activates it. One special per charge cycle: Mega 30s / Dynamax 20s / one Z burst; a successful rift return reopens charging.
